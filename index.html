Why Chog matters · Trait → Sound

Chog traits shape each voice.

The six embedded sample Chogs have explicit sound mappings for their Background, Eyes, and Body values. Compare the traits and eight-note phrase below; unfamiliar values use deterministic fallbacks.

Current tokens · A + B

Sound map: For the six embedded samples, Background selects an explicit tonal palette; Eyes select a melody contour, default rhythm, and waveform; Body selects the note envelope (a missing Body trait uses a neutral mapping). Other Background, Eyes, and Body values use deterministic fallbacks. Side pans the voice; any Aura adds a quiet shimmer on steps 1 and 5. A live-token ID plus Head and Mouth can rotate its phrase contour by 0–2 steps. Starter art is a token snapshot; Monad lookup is read-only and never verifies ownership. Tier is descriptive metadata only; it does not make a voice louder or better.
